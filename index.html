Good to know

Questions, answered.

What are laundry detergent sheets?

Thin sheets of concentrated, plant-based detergent with advanced multi-enzymes. They dissolve completely in the wash and replace liquid and powder detergent — no measuring, no spills and no heavy plastic bottles.

How do I use a dr.glo laundry sheet?

Place one sheet in the drum before adding your clothes, then wash as usual. For large or heavily soiled loads, use two sheets.

Are dr.glo sheets safe for abayas and delicate clothes?

Yes. Our formula is plant-based, with no phosphates, no chlorine and no artificial colourants, and it is safe for all clothes and abayas. For black clothes and abayas, Black Magic helps keep the colour deep black.

Where can I buy dr.glo in Bahrain?

dr.glo is available at 57 points of sale across every governorate of Bahrain, including Danube, Al Helli, Tamimi Markets and Mega Mart, and online at drgloshop.com with delivery within 2 days.

Is dr.glo available in Saudi Arabia?

Yes. From our operations base in the Eastern Province, dr.glo is available at 27 points of sale through partners including Mazaya, Al Muntazah Markets and Al-A'raf Markets, with delivery to all regions within 3–5 days.

Are the sheets good for travel?

Very. dr.glo sheets are light, compact and can't spill, so you can pack a few for any trip.
